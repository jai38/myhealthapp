# M02 v1.1 · Medication Details — revised screen specification

**Status:** REVIEW (spec only; no visual design) · **Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Revises:** M02 v1.0 (design in review since 2026-11-19; never locked). Everything in v1.0 that isn’t listed in §1 stays as drawn.
**Reason:** S-RX-04 OPEN-7 ("align M02 Start Taking with Sheet U and 4B before M02 is locked") and the screens locked since 2026-11-22 that open M02 (S-RX-04 v1.0/v1.1, R-DOC-02).
**Sources (APPROVED):** M02 v1.0 content (screens.md, `api.md` `MedicationDetail`); S-RX-04 v1.0 §5 (Paused earlier → Review Restart; Stopped or completed earlier → Review New Course; As needed; Reaction on record), **Sheet U**, **Sheet W**, **4B** (start date, empty dose slots, prescribed timing as a hint only, reminders off by default, permission notice; never pre-filled from unverified values), OPEN-1 (no tracking for a medicine with a recorded reaction until the clinical gate), OPEN-5 (private reminder text); **S-RX-04 v1.1** §4 4E (Review stop / Review pause: what will happen, From date today and editable, optional reason, never back-dated automatically, no resume date, never restarts by itself) and §6 (M02 shows "Stopped on 23 Nov · Reason: As per Apollo Hospital discharge summary", "Paused since 23 Nov" with Resume Medicine); **R-DOC-02** §3.5 (medicine rows open M02), §4 (tracked medicine affected → Review Medicine); RX-MAT-07, 08, 09, 11, 15, 18; RX-VER-06 (correction never silently changes a schedule); rules 10–15; S06/S07 (Taken only by explicit action; Not confirmed ≠ Missed).
**Labels:** APPROVED · PROPOSED (needs approval) · OPEN.

## 2. States (v1.0 + new)
`active` · `manual` · `paused` · `stopped` · `inactive` (from v1.0) · **`asneeded`** · **`reaction`** (inactive with a recorded reaction) · **`corrected`** (active, source record changed) · **`stopsheet`** · **`starttaking`**.

## 3. Start Taking (inactive; APPROVED Sheet U + 4B)
1. **Sheet U:** "Are you taking Metformin now?" **Yes, I’m Taking It** · **No, I’m Not** (stays Not in daily list) · **Not Sure** (stays; nothing changes). The answer is saved with the time and never changes tracking by itself (APPROVED RX-MAT-11).
2. **4B Set up tracking** as a full-screen sheet: From the prescription (read-only, source link) · Start (today, editable) · **Dose times — empty slots**, prescribed timing as a hint ("after breakfast") · End (from the prescription or No end date) · Reminders switch **off by default**, permission notice if blocked. Never pre-filled from unverified values (APPROVED MF-D07).
3. **Confirm** (single change, so no 4D list): "**Start tracking Metformin 500 mg?** From 24 Nov · 8:00 AM, 8:00 PM · reminders on" · **Start Tracking** · **Back**. One change only, idempotent (APPROVED RX-MAT-20).
4. After: status ● Active; Today shows its doses from the start date; toast "Metformin added to your daily list".

If the source item is **not verified**, Start Taking is replaced by "**Check it first** — the strength on the prescription isn’t confirmed yet." · **Continue Verification** (→ R-DOC-02 → S-RX-03 at that item) (APPROVED RX-VER-03).

## 4. Sources (PROPOSED)
Card **Sources** replaces v1.0’s Prescription / Source card:
- One row per source, newest first: "Prescription · Dr. Rao · 22 Nov 2026" · "Discharge summary · Apollo Hospital · 7 Nov 2026" · "Added by you · 2 Aug 2026". Each opens R-DOC-02 (restricted sources show "Restricted record" and need Face ID).
- Under the newest prescribed source: **As written** in the grey code-style pill, verbatim (`nameAsWritten`), "Exactly as on the prescription" (v1.0, APPROVED rule 12).
- "View Prescription" is removed here; the original is opened from R-DOC-02 (one canonical record, APPROVED RX-VER-13).

## 5. Stop and pause (APPROVED S-RX-04 v1.1 4E pattern)
**Stop Medicine** → "**Stop tracking Aspirin?**":
- What will happen: "Reminders for Aspirin stop" · "Future doses are removed from Today" · "Your history stays, including doses you logged" · "You can start it again later as a new course".
- **From** date: today, editable, never back-dated automatically.
- **Reason** (optional, free text; pre-filled only when the stop came from a document, e.g. "As per Apollo Hospital discharge summary, 7 Nov 2026").
- **Stop Medicine** (red) · **Cancel**. Commits on tap (single change); toast "Aspirin stopped · history kept".

**Pause Medicine** → "**Pause Metformin?**": "Reminders pause" · "No doses on Today while paused" · "It won’t restart by itself — you resume it when your doctor says" · From date · optional reason · **Pause Medicine** · **Cancel**. No resume date (APPROVED RX-MAT-07).

Status notes stay as v1.0; a stop or pause from a document shows its reason with a link: "Reason: As per Apollo Hospital discharge summary" → R-DOC-02 (APPROVED v1.1 §6).

## 6. Start a New Course (stopped / completed; APPROVED RX-MAT-08 "Review New Course")
Manage card for stopped/completed shows only **Start a New Course** → 4B with "Your earlier course and its history stay as they are." and start date today → confirm "Start a new course of Aspirin 75 mg?" → **Start Tracking**. The old course stays Stopped/Completed in History; the new one is a separate course (PROPOSED; backend flag).

## 7. As needed (APPROVED RX-MAT-15)
- Chips: ● Active · **As needed** (grey, icon + word).
- No Today card and no daily doses; instead **Log a Dose** (primary) → time sheet (Just now / Other time, not in the future, S07 pattern) → toast "Dose of Paracetamol logged at 2:10 PM" with Undo.
- How to take: "As needed · as written: “1 tablet if fever, max 3 a day”" — the limit is shown verbatim only; Ayoreni doesn’t count or warn (OPEN in RX-MAT-15; PROPOSED: no counting).
- History: logged doses only; never "Missed" or "Not confirmed" (nothing was scheduled).
- Reminders: "None for as-needed medicines".

## 8. Reaction on record (APPROVED Sheet W, S-RX-04 OPEN-1)
- Top card (amber, not dismissable): "**A reaction is recorded for this medicine**" · Suspected or Confirmed (never merged) · symptoms and date · **View Reaction**.
- For an inactive medicine, **Start Taking** is replaced by: "Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before you start tracking it." No tracking from here until the clinical gate decides (APPROVED OPEN-1).
- For an already active medicine, the card shows; Manage stays available (stopping is always allowed).
- Ayoreni never says a medicine is safe because no reaction is recorded (APPROVED).

## 9. Record corrected (APPROVED RX-VER-06, R-DOC-02 §4)
When a source item this course uses was edited and needs review: banner under the header "**The prescription for this medicine was corrected.** Your tracking hasn’t changed. Review it to decide." · **Review Change** → S-RX-04 Compare / Review Change for this medicine (before → after strip). Nothing changes on Today or in reminders until confirmed. Banner clears when decided (Keep Existing or Review Change confirmed).

## 10. Copy notes (PROPOSED)
- Reminder text on the lock screen: "Time for your medicine" unless the person turned on names (APPROVED OPEN-5).
- Inactive note keeps v1.0 copy: "This came from an older prescription, so it has no reminders. Start it only if you’re taking it now."
