**Status:** REVIEW (spec only; no visual design) · **Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Changes:** the locked S06 Today (2026-11-16) and S05 Welcome / first-time Today (2026-11-16) — **one conditional row only**; nothing else on either screen changes.

## 2. The row (PROPOSED layout, APPROVED copy)
Position: directly under the hero, above the first time group (S06) or above Quick Actions (S05), with the standard 16 pt gap; a glass card row (radius 22, min height 68), full width.
- Icon tile (icon chip radius 12, brand-tint): document with a check mark.
- Title **Records to Review** (Headline 17/22).
- Subtitle (Meta 14–15/20): "**3 documents require verification**" / "**1 document requires verification**" (APPROVED RX-VER-09 examples).
- Right: chevron. No count badge, no colour-only signal, no amber (it is not an alert).
- Whole row is one button; screen reader: "Records to Review, 3 documents require verification, button".

**Tap →** R-DOC-01 Documents with **Pending Verification** selected; Back returns to Today (APPROVED R-DOC-01 §2).

**Not dismissible** (PROPOSED, OD-2): it disappears by itself when nothing is pending, and stays out of the way otherwise.

## 3. Counting and visibility (APPROVED RX-VER-09)
- Count = unique saved documents for the **selected profile** with status Unverified or Partially Verified. Not items, fields, pages or reminders (VER-AC-09.1: two prescriptions with ten open fields → "2 documents").
- Restricted records count like any other but are never named or hinted at (APPROVED DOC-08 §4).
- Documents still uploading or being read are not counted (nothing saved yet; APPROVED S-RX-03 save boundary).
- Generated doctor PDF reports are never counted (APPROVED D-RX-3).
- Updates when a record is verified, saved for later, reopened by an edit, deleted, or the profile changes (VER-AC-09.2–09.4). At 0 the row is removed and the space returns to the day’s content.

| Largest text size | Title and subtitle wrap; row grows; nothing clipped |

## 5. Data and privacy
Reads the pending count only (server-derived from canonical document ids and status; APPROVED). Writes nothing. The row and count never appear in notifications, widgets or on the lock screen (PROPOSED). No document details on Today.
